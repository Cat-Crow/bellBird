# bellBird
A time awareness app. bellBird allows your phone to play a pleasant chime on a recurring schedule, even when the phone is on silent/vibrate or Do Not Disturb mode.

Unlike some of the other time awareness apps, bellBird's two chimes can be set independently of each other and do not need to keep to a one hour cycle. The tones' volumes are based on your phone's alarm volume, but can be adjusted via the app.

A minimalist Android app with no data collected. Useful for meditation, focus sessions, or simply staying aware of time passing.

Features
- Two independent timers (primary and secondary) with highly configurable intervals.
- Five synthesized tones: Soft Bell, High Bell, Deep Bell, Double Chime, Bowl. The tones were chosen to be audible but not disruptive.
- Volume control per timer.
- Quiet hours with overnight wrap-around.
- Do Not Disturb integration with optional override.
- Optional vibration.
- No internet access, no tracking, no ads.

Requirements
Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later.

Installation
Download the latest APK from the Releases (https://github.com/Cat-Crow/bellBird/releases) page and sideload it. On your device, enable Install from unknown sources for your browser or file manager, then open the downloaded file.
